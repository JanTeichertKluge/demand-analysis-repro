\section{Robustness Check: Embeddings without Fine-Tuning}\label{app:gemma}

\revised{This section repeats the analysis with off-the-shelf embeddings that are not fine-tuned. The workflow is exactly the same as in the main analysis (Section~\ref{app:workflow}), except that the fine-tuning in step~2 is omitted, the embeddings in step~3 are computed with EmbeddingGemma~2 \citep{embeddinggemma2}, and their truncation to 256 coordinates replaces the random projection in Algorithm~\ref{alg:features}. All other steps of Algorithm~\ref{alg:features}, the sample split, the estimation sample, the tabular controls, the learners, and the random seeds are unchanged.}

\revised{EmbeddingGemma~2 is an open multimodal embedding model released by Google DeepMind in October 2026. Built on Gemma 4, it maps text, images, video, and audio into a shared 768-dimensional space. Of its 740M parameters, we use the 270M-parameter text model and the 170M-parameter vision encoder. The description and the image of each product are encoded jointly, with the task prefix for clustering (checkpoint \texttt{google/embeddinggemma-2}, revision \texttt{<<revision>>}). As the model is trained with Matryoshka representation learning, we keep the leading 256 coordinates of each embedding and re-normalize them. Unlike the fine-tuned embeddings, these embeddings are not trained on our data and use neither tabular inputs nor the price and quantity signals. The comparison therefore changes the encoders, their inputs, and the fine-tuning at the same time. Figures~\ref{fig:gemma_ate} and \ref{fig:gemma_sorted} also show two variants: text-only embeddings (256 dimensions) and text and image embeddings with all 768 dimensions.}
